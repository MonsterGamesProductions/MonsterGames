About Bike Stunt Game

Description

Are you a fearless stunt rider? Get on your motorbike and take on impossible tracks and mega ramps built high in the sky, with no limit on speed.

Perform crazy air stunts and 360 flips, race against other riders, and prove you have the skills to become the ultimate bike stunt champion.

Operating System & Device Support

Bike Stunt Game is built to run across a wide range of devices:

iOS: iPhone, iPads

Android: Android smartphones and tablets

Rating & Store Info: Free to download with ads and in-app purchases

Input Control Support

Simple controls let you focus on landing every stunt:

On-screen button controls

Accelerate, brake and lean controls

Features & Recent Highlights

Jump, flip and race your way across the most impossible tracks in the sky.

  • 1: Mega Ramp Tracks: Ride impossible tracks and huge ramps high in the sky.
  • 2: Crazy Air Stunts: Pull off 360 flips and jaw-dropping air stunts.
  • 3: Multiplayer Racing: Race against other riders on stunt tracks.
  • 4: Unlockable Bikes: Earn coins and unlock new stunt bikes.
  • 5: Realistic 3D Graphics: Stunning 3D visuals and bike physics.
  • 6: Challenging Levels: Levels get harder as you master your riding skills.
