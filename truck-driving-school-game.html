About Truck Driving School Game

Description

Learn to drive big rigs and earn your trucking license. Start as a beginner and work through driving challenges on city streets, mountain roads and more, while watching for traffic, road signs and hazards.

Upgrade and customize your truck's engine, wheels, paint and decals. The latest update adds a career truck job mode, new truck models and bigger, redesigned environments.
