About My Pizza Shop Simulator

Description

Start with a small pizza shop and grow it into a food empire. Cook and serve customers at the counter and the drive-thru, keep them happy, and earn cash.

Hire and upgrade waiters, cashiers and cleaners, improve your kitchen and interiors, and expand into coffee, burger and ice cream shops on your way to becoming a food business tycoon.

Operating System & Device Support

My Pizza Shop Simulator is built to run across a wide range of devices:

iOS: iPhone, iPads, and Apple silicon Macs

Android: Android smartphones and tablets

Rating & Store Info: Rated 4+ on the App Store; free to download with ads and in-app purchases

Input Control Support

Easy tap controls let you run your shop:

Tap to cook and serve

Tap to hire and upgrade

Simple menu navigation

Features & Recent Highlights

Bake, serve and build your way to food business tycoon.

  • 1: Run Your Pizza Shop: Serve customers at the counter and the drive-thru.
  • 2: Hire & Upgrade Staff: Waiters, cashiers and cleaners to help your business grow.
  • 3: Expand Your Business: Open coffee, burger and ice cream shops.
  • 4: Upgrade Everything: Improve interiors, equipment and staff skills.
  • 5: VIP Customers: Serve regular and VIP customers and respond to their feedback.
  • 6: Leaderboards & Achievements: Unlock new regions and stores, with Game Center leaderboards and achievements.
