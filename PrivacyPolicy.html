Privacy Policy

Monster Games Productions PTY LTD

Last Updated: 14 November 2026

1. INTRODUCTION

Monster Games Productions PTY LTD ("Company", "we", "us", or "our") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the information of users ("you" or "your") who download, install, access, or play our mobile games and applications ("Games").

This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected when you use our Games, how that information is used, how it may be shared with third parties, and what choices and rights you may have regarding your information.

Our Games are distributed through third-party platforms, including:

  • Apple App Store
  • Google Play Store
  • Amazon Appstore
  • Samsung Galaxy Store

Our Games may include advertising, analytics, in-app purchases, subscriptions, cloud-based services, and other third-party technologies.

By using our Games, you acknowledge that you have had the opportunity to read this Privacy Policy.

If you do not agree with this Privacy Policy, please discontinue using our Games.

Privacy Policy URL: http://monstergamesproductions.com.au/PrivacyPolicy.html

2. INFORMATION WE COLLECT

Depending on the Game, the device you use, your settings, your consent choices, and the third-party services integrated into the Game, certain information may be collected automatically or provided by you.

We do not necessarily collect every category of information listed below in every Game.

2.1 Device and Technical Information

Our Games and integrated third-party SDKs may collect technical information, including:

  • Device manufacturer and model.
  • Device operating system and version.
  • Device language and regional settings.
  • Screen resolution and device specifications.
  • IP address.
  • Mobile advertising identifiers, where available and permitted.
  • App version and game version.
  • Network connection information.
  • General location information estimated from an IP address.
  • Device performance and diagnostic information.
  • Crash reports and error logs.

This information may be used to operate our Games, provide advertisements, analyze performance, prevent fraud, and improve user experience.

2.2 Advertising Information

Our advertising partners may collect information such as:

  • Advertising identifiers.
  • Information about advertisements displayed.
  • Advertisement views, clicks, and interactions.
  • General device and technical information.
  • IP address and approximate location.
  • Information used to determine advertising preferences.
  • Information used to measure advertising effectiveness.

Depending on your location, consent choices, device settings, and applicable laws, advertising may be personalized or non-personalized.

2.3 Gameplay and Analytics Information

Through Firebase Analytics and other integrated services, information about your interaction with our Games may be collected, including:

  • Game launches and sessions.
  • Gameplay events and interactions.
  • Levels completed and game progress, where configured.
  • In-game purchases and related events.
  • Advertising interactions.
  • Game settings and preferences, where configured.
  • App performance and diagnostic events.

We use this information to understand how our Games are used, improve gameplay, identify technical issues, and develop new features.

2.4 Information Stored Through Firebase Firestore

Some of our Games use Google Firebase Cloud Firestore.

Depending on the features implemented in a particular Game, Firestore may be used to store game-related information, such as:

  • Game progress and saved data.
  • Player settings and preferences.
  • In-game statistics.
  • Game-related identifiers.
  • Other information necessary to provide cloud-based game features.

The actual information stored depends on the individual Game and its functionality.

Firestore data is processed and stored using Google's Firebase infrastructure. Access to stored information is restricted through appropriate technical and security controls.

2.5 Information You Provide

If a Game provides features allowing you to contact us, submit feedback, or communicate with us, we may collect information you voluntarily provide, such as:

  • Your email address.
  • Your message or feedback.
  • Information relating to a support request.

Please do not submit sensitive personal information unless it is necessary for your request.

Our Games may offer optional in-app purchases and paid subscriptions.

We use Unity In-App Purchasing (Unity IAP) version 5.4.1 to integrate in-app purchasing functionality.

Purchases may include:

  • Virtual currency.
  • Additional game content.
  • Game upgrades.
  • Premium features.
  • Subscription-based services.
  • Other optional digital items.

Payments and transactions are processed through the applicable application store, such as Apple App Store, Google Play, Amazon Appstore, or Samsung Galaxy Store.

We do not directly receive or store your complete payment card details through Unity IAP.

The relevant application store may process payment information according to its own privacy policy and terms.

We may receive limited purchase-related information, such as transaction status, product identifiers, purchase validation information, or subscription status, as necessary to provide purchased content and services.

For information about payment processing, please consult the privacy policy of the relevant application store.
