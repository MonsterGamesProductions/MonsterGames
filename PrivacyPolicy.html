1. INTRODUCTION

Monster Games Productions PTY LTD ("Monster Games Productions", "Company", "We", "Us" or "Our") is an Australian mobile game development and publishing company.

We develop and distribute mobile games through third-party application stores, including:

• Apple App Store

• Google Play Store

• Amazon Appstore

• Samsung Galaxy Store

This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used, processed, stored and shared when you download, install or play our mobile games and use related services (collectively referred to as our "Games" or "Services").

Our Games may integrate third-party technologies and services for advertising, advertising mediation, analytics, cloud storage, authentication, gaming features, crash reporting and in-app purchases.

These services may include Google Firebase, Google AdMob, AppLovin MAX, Unity Technologies, Google Play Games Services, Apple Game Center and other third-party providers described in this policy.

Not every Game uses every service described in this Privacy Policy. The services and technologies used may differ depending on the individual Game, its platform, configuration and available features.

We aim to collect and use information only where reasonably necessary for operating, maintaining, supporting and improving our Games and Services.

Where applicable law requires consent for particular processing activities, we use appropriate consent mechanisms. Simply downloading or playing a Game does not, by itself, constitute consent to processing where separate consent is legally required.

If you do not agree with this Privacy Policy, you should discontinue using our Games.

Monster Games Productions PTY LTD is responsible for the information it collects and processes through its own services and business operations, subject to applicable privacy laws.

Some of our Games use Firebase Authentication with anonymous sign-in.

Anonymous authentication allows players to use certain online features without creating a traditional account using their real name, email address or password.

Firebase may generate a unique anonymous user identifier (UID) to recognize a player and support Game functionality.

This identifier may be used to:

• Save game progress.

• Support cloud-save functionality.

• Associate gameplay records with a player.

• Provide online Game features.

• Maintain player-related Game information.

Anonymous authentication does not mean that no information is processed. Anonymous identifiers may still be considered personal information or pseudonymous information under applicable privacy laws.

Some of our Games use Firebase Firestore, a cloud-hosted database service provided by Google.

Depending on the individual Game, information stored through Firestore may include:

• Game progress and saved gameplay data.

• Game settings and preferences.

• Game-related records.

• Other information necessary for online functionality.

The information stored depends on the features implemented in each Game.

We do not require players to provide their real name, email address or password for the anonymous authentication functionality described above.
