About City Taxi Simulator

Description

Take the wheel as a city taxi driver. Ride requests can come in at any moment while you cruise the streets, so accept the job fast and reach your passenger before other drivers beat you to it. Great service and on-time pickups earn you bigger rewards and tips.

Handle early-morning airport runs where every second counts, then leave the city behind for offroad taxi driving in the countryside, where an online rival may try to steal your fares. City Taxi Simulator brings a realistic, detailed taxi driving experience to your device.

Operating System & Device Support

City Taxi Simulator is built to run across a wide range of devices and input setups:

iOS: iPhone, iPads

Android: Android smartphones, Android tablets, Chromebooks, and Android TV

Windows: Windows PCs and laptops (Google Play Games on PC)

TV & Large Screens: Android TV support with gamepad and keyboard input

Rating & Store Info: Rated 3+ on Google Play; free to download with ads and in-app purchases (including optional Pro membership)

Features & Recent Highlights

Pick up passengers, follow the GPS, and become the best taxi driver in town.

  • 1: Wide Selection of Taxis: Drive everything from classic taxi cars to modern taxi models.
  • 2: Phone Booking: Receive ride requests on your phone and race to pick up your passengers.
  • 3: GPS Navigation: Follow road navigation to reach every pickup and destination.
  • 4: Smart AI Traffic: Share the road with intelligent cars, vans, and trucks.
  • 5: Big City & Offroad Environments: Explore a large city and drive offroad taxi routes in rural areas.
  • 6: Flexible Controls & HD Graphics: Steering, buttons, tilt, and joystick controls with high-quality graphics.
