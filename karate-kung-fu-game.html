About Karate Kung Fu Game

Description

Step into the ring in this martial arts fighting game. Master karate, kung fu, boxing and kickboxing moves and take on tough opponents.

Fight in realistic fight club environments powered by a smooth game engine, and become the ultimate martial arts champion in your free time.

iOS: iPhone, iPads

Android: Android smartphones and tablets

Rating & Store Info: Rated 3+ on Google Play; free to download with ads

On-screen movement controls
