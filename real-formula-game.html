About Real Formula Car Racing Games

Description

As a Formula racer driver, you dream about winning your first race of your life. Are you desperate to know what it feels like winning your first formula race? Find it out for yourself.

Hop onto the driving seat of your formula racing car, get onto state-of-the-art tracks across the globe, and prove yourself on the circuit. Experience cars rushing past each other to the finish line in high-speed formula car racing action.

Operating System & Device Support

Real Formula Car Racing Games is built to run across a wide range of devices and input setups:

iOS: iPhone, iPads

Fire OS: Fire OS: Fire OS devices, Fire TV

Windows: Windows PCs and laptops

Android: Android smartphones, Android tablets, and Android TV

TV & Large Screens: Remote controls input support for TV

Rating & Store Info: Rated Everyone on Google Play; free to download with in-app purchases and ads (including optional weekly plan)

Features & Recent Highlights

Compete across global circuits, earn trophies, and push your Formula car to the finish line.

  • 1: Real-Time Multiplayer: Play freely with players from around the world in real time.
  • 2: 20 Tracks Across 20 Countries: Race across 20 tracks divided into 3 difficulty levels and track lengths.
  • 3: Dynamic Conditions & Times: Race during daytime, sunset, and night-time on desert and rainy tracks.
  • 4: Trophies, Achievements & Leaderboards: Earn bronze, silver, and gold trophies on each track, unlock 3 achievements, and climb 3 leaderboards.
  • 5: Modern Formula Model & Speedometer: Experience the Formula Car Model 2025 with a sleek, modern speedometer and multiple graphics modes.
  • 6: Flexible Controls: Smooth tilt, button, and steering controls alongside keyboard/mouse, gamepad, and TV remote support.
